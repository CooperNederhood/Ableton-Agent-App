# MIDI composition

Inspect the target track and clip before writing notes. Establish the requested
bar range, meter, register, and musical role. Prefer a small coherent motif over
unstructured note volume. Preserve existing material unless replacement is
explicitly requested, and verify note count, timing, pitch range, and clip
length after editing.
